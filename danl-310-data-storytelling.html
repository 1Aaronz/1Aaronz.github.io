This project visualizes Global High Speed Rail (HSR) progress compared to the US.

Your project should tell an interesting and coherent story using:

  • descriptive statistics,
  • data transformation, and
  • data visualization.

A literature review, regression model, machine learning model, or formal statistical model is not required.

Big Idea

Despite public interest in HSR in the US, 60% of U.S. voters have a favorable opinion of high-speed rail Only 16% view it unfavorably (Public Policy Polling), the US has fallen behind in HSR construction when compared to countries that have built HSR.

This project emphasizes the difference in the length of HSR other countries have managed to achieve, and are projected to achieve in the next 10 years while the US continues to have slow progress in HSR construction.
